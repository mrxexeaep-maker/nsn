- With several layers selected, the handles on their selection box resize them all together and its rotation handle turns them about its center, as in Figma: each layer keeps its place in the box and scales along its own axes, a rotated layer keeps its angle, Shift keeps proportions or snaps the angle, and one undo puts the whole change back. The box turns to the angle the layers share, and after a rotation it stays turned while the selection lasts.
